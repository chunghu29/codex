Inspired beginnings for a lifetime of learning

Feifei’s Little Ivy Club was founded by educators who believe every child deserves a gentle and enriching introduction to the world. Our curriculum blends Montessori-inspired independence, Reggio Emilia creativity, and research-backed bilingual practices.

We honor each child’s interests with responsive lesson plans, sensory exploration, and storytelling that reflects both Chinese and global cultures. Daily routines prioritize safety, healthy meals, and meaningful outdoor play.

Highlights

  • Mandarin & English certified educators
  • Nutritious meals prepared onsite
  • STEM tinkering, music, and art studios

Why bilingual immersion in the early years?

Research shows that dual-language learning strengthens problem solving, social-emotional growth, and cultural empathy. At Little Ivy, bilingualism is woven into every interaction.

Cognitive Flexibility

Children learn to switch between Mandarin and English seamlessly, strengthening memory and critical thinking.

Deep Cultural Roots

Storytelling, cooking, and celebrations build pride in Chinese heritage while welcoming diverse backgrounds.

Family Connection

Language-rich updates keep caregivers involved, empowering families to continue learning at home.
